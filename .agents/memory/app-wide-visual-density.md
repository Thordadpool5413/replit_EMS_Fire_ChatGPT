description: User-stated sizing preference for the EMS & Fire Territory Intelligence app across screens.
---

Keep the entire app compact, clean, and scannable on laptop and phone. Reduce oversized cards, padding, spacing, typography, and persistent chrome that make screens feel overwhelming, but do not shrink text or controls until they become hard to read or use. Preserve the map-only Home screen, every feature, and all available data.

**Why:** The user said the full app looked too large and overwhelming on a laptop and asked for a cleaner experience throughout the app.

**How to apply:** Tune shared shell and component density consistently across workspaces; check laptop and phone layouts, maintain readable type and tap targets, and keep all secondary information accessible rather than deleting it.
