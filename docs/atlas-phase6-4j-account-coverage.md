# Phase 6.4J — Account activity coverage

Read-only, authenticated selected-account territory command view. 30/60/90-day inclusive local-date windows; distinct eligible selected account denominator; explicitly recorded contact (site visit, ride-along, phone call, virtual demo), separate field visits, most recent recorded dates, older and missing records, due/overdue tasks, reviewed commercial priorities and source-matched location availability. Standalone tasks, RFP desk reviews, malformed/future dates, duplicated IDs and ambiguous source identities never count as contact. A missing record never proves absence of contact. No territory ownership, national market share or company-level penetration claims; eight-account selected scope only and five-minute reviewed sample. No automatic task creation, provider write, external communication, new backend or storage. Existing profile and field planner entry points retained. Regression fixtures test-only. Replit deployment remains user-controlled.
