---
name: County Medicare denial matching
description: Source and calculation rules for displaying Medicare claim-denial metrics by county.
---

For an actual county Medicare claim-denial rate, use a direct county-level report matched by the canonical five-digit county FIPS. Assign ambulance claims by pickup/service county, not the provider's billing or headquarters address. Define the unit (unique claims or claim lines) and decision point (initial or final after adjustment/appeal); keep those measures separate and use the same unit in numerator and denominator. For final claim-level rates, deduplicate adjustment/reversal transactions to one final disposition per claim. When raw counts are available, calculate denied claims divided by adjudicated claims for the same claim scope and reporting period. For any claim-count rollup, sum denied counts and divide by summed adjudicated counts only when scope and period match; never average ZIP percentages into a county claims rate.

Do not expose ZIP-profile values as denial percentages unless each value has an original source URL, reporting period, and claim scope. The reviewed nationwide ZIP registry has hard-coded percentages without any of those fields, and exact-value searches did not identify original records. Keep those values out of denial-rate displays until traceable records are supplied. CMS ambulance AFS ZIP files describe payment geography and fee schedule amounts; Market Saturation & Utilization reports paid-claim use at state/county level; neither validates ZIP denial percentages.

When source-backed ZIP-profile percentages are available, show each value as one scalar, link its source, and display its period and claim scope. A state profile summary may use an unweighted arithmetic mean only when every included value shares the same reporting period and claim scope; show the number and individual ZIP values. Label it as a ZIP-profile average, not a claim-count-weighted state denial rate. Provider-profile percentages may remain visible as single values when present, but label them as profile values unless claim-specific evidence supports a CMS label.

A claim-count denial percentage is not a denied-dollar percentage. Do not estimate denied dollars by multiplying billed dollars by a claim denial rate; use a reported denied-dollar amount for that same claim cohort.

**Why:** CMS Medicare Geographic Variation county data reports Medicare utilization and spending, not ambulance claim-denial counts. CMS Market Saturation & Utilization State-County includes paid-claim utilization (users, providers, and FFS beneficiaries), but no denied/adjudicated counts. CMS AFS ZIP data is for payment calculations. None supports the unattributed ZIP denial percentages in the app.

**How to apply:** Show a true county claims rate only when its county-level measure and reporting scope are available. A ZIP profile is a separate fallback only when it has a source, period, and claim scope; do not substitute it for the county rate. Suppress unsupported ZIP values and do not show a state summary if the included profiles are missing or mix periods/scopes. Calculate financial recovery only from reported denied-dollar amounts, not a count-based rate; never label Medicare payment or utilization metrics as denials.
