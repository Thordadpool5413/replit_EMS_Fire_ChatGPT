# Phase 6.5A — Leadership account review

Read-only synthesis of existing selected-account reviewed evidence, 30/60/90-day recorded activity, source location availability, due tasks and the signed-in user's browser-local working pipeline. Distinct denominators; missing or unreadable working records are separate from confirmed empty records. Seven overlapping review prompts in a fixed transparent sequence, source links, navigation to existing account/profile/field-plan workflows. No team or rep scorecards, company-wide metrics, role elevation, revenue forecast, CRM changes, export of private notes or automatic task creation. Current scope is at most eight eligible selected accounts and a five-minute reviewed sample. Shared manager reporting requires future server-backed opportunity persistence, assignment and manager authorization. Regression fixtures test-only. Replit deployment remains user-controlled.
