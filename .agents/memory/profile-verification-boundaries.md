---
name: Profile verification boundaries
description: Required separation of imported agency research, individual fact reviews, and user corrections.
---

Individual EMS and fire profiles must retain useful unconfirmed research while making the user's responsibility to independently verify every fact explicit. A source type and a user's review status are separate.

**Why:** The user supplied a profile-only build directive requiring source inspection, individual confirmations, corrections, discrepancy flags, notes and history without automatically certifying imported information.

**How to apply:** Confirm individual fields, never whole equipment groups: model confirmation must not confirm quantity, age, warranty, dates or supplier. Preserve original values and reviewer/date/method history. Retain browser-local storage until the existing workspace access boundary is verified; label self-entered reviewer identity honestly. Corrections must not silently alter imported records or financial calculations.
