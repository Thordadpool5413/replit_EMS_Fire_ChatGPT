# Phase 7.0E — Procurement and contract research

Integrates the existing validated Chicago municipal purchase order connector into the selected-state national research workflow for Illinois. One bounded 25-row page over the most recent 365 days, alongside existing federal assistance and supplier awards; NYC remains a separate 90-day notice connector. Purchase order number and revision remain distinct source-native rows. Negative/zero modifications are shown without aggregation or inferred renewal. Adds conservative notice lifecycle labels and filtering, distinguishing solicitations (status unconfirmed), elapsed/today deadlines, intent to award, published awards, supplier awards, grants and purchase orders. No auto-association of suppliers or municipal publishers with providers. SAM.gov remains a separate credential-gated connector. No new data source beyond existing Chicago connector, no live records collected, no nationwide local procurement coverage claim, no installed-fleet inference or pipeline writes. End-user validation of original notices and amendments required. Replit unchanged. Synthetic fixtures confined to tests.
