## Department billing records already mapped

- Naperville’s [2026 city report](https://naperville.legistar.com/ViewReport.ashx?Extra=WithText&GID=682&GUID=D35006D7-4748-4CE3-ACFD-6D42B9E6275F&ID=8027168&M=R&N=Master&Title=Legislation+Details+%28With+Text%29) includes an in-house pilot with 316 administered claims and 47 initial denials. The pilot represents about 20% of billable EMS calls. It also reports billed and collected dollars separately.
- Alameda’s [department study](https://www.alamedaca.gov/files/sharedassets/public/v/1/fire/fire/annual-reports/alameda-crasocstudy-final.pdf) gives 2022 trip shares by payer, plus department transport charges and payments.
- Saint Charles’s [billing RFP addendum](https://stcharlescitymo.gov/DocumentCenter/View/13250/4747-Addendum-1?bidId=) gives the percentage of billed charges for four payer groups in 2022 and 2023. The reported percentages are preserved without redistributing the unreported balance.

Claim counts, trips, billed charges and collections keep their source denominators. For additional municipalities, billing RFPs, council reports, city audits and department standards-of-cover studies are sources of actual named-agency figures. For payer-specific denials and current figures where public evidence is absent, use authorized aggregate billing exports.

## Nationwide publication

The app now loads three public-source snapshots from its state, county and provider equipment views. Provider views use a source-backed county where present, a NERIS headquarters county when its boundary or registry crosswalk supports one, and otherwise the state. This research panel is separate from agency-owned equipment and departmental purchasing records.

| Feed | Nationwide coverage | Geographic basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OpenStreetMap AED objects | 2,895 distinct mapped objects on November 27, 2026. 2,855 mapped to 366 counties or equivalents. 40 remained unresolved. The query excludes locations explicitly tagged private or no access; some other access conditions remain. | OpenStreetMap objects tagged `emergency=defibrillator`. County from the mapped point or geometric center inside one generalized county boundary. Includes a count for every county in the app’s 50-state and D.C. reference geography. |
| USAspending defibrillator award search | All 50 states and D.C. queried. 377 candidates in 65 recipient counties. The published snapshot captures the first 25 awards per state, sorted by award amount, from 2024 through November 27, 2026. | The recipient’s address controls the geographic assignment. The county is not a delivery location or buyer identifier. Keyword results remain research leads. |
| GovSentry portal directory | 138 state and local portal links across all 50 states and D.C. | State directory only. Each portal is a place to look for the underlying purchase. Directory by [GovSentry](https://govsentry.ai/developers), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), adapted to select unique HTTPS links. |

The GitHub workflow **National AED and procurement source refresh** fetches the three feeds and packages new snapshots as reviewable build inputs. Publishing fresh snapshots requires updating the bundled files and importing the resulting Git revision into Replit. To update the coverage CSVs and regenerate the NERIS headquarters county lookup from the repository root:

```sh
./scripts/node_modules/.bin/tsx scripts/map-provider-source-coverage.mts /absolute/output artifacts/ems-territory-intelligence/public/data/neris-headquarters-counties.json
```

An authorized user can load aggregate all-payer records from a saved provider's Claims workspace. The CSV requires an exact native or confirmed registry key, reporting periods and standard payer categories. It accepts aggregate claim counts, adjudicated counts, initial and final denied counts, charges and payments. Percentages use only the relevant denominators. The importer is now backed by private database revisions, as described below. CMS public utilization and its ground-ambulance survey do not provide a nationwide agency-denial or all-payer export.

## Durable provider source records

Saved profiles with an active native registry identity now have three separate private panels. Equipment accepts a dated provider-owned register by finished product make, model and scope. Purchasing accepts dated event rows with source references, supplier and amounts where documented. Claims accepts an authorized aggregate export, with payer, reporting period, cohort and denial definition. An adjudicated denominator is required to calculate an initial or final denial rate. Missing fields remain blank, and each displayed zero comes from a source value of zero.

An authorized workspace editor previews and saves a new snapshot. Workspace viewers can reload the latest revision and inspect earlier revisions. Writes retain prior revisions, and the server checks the saved provider's source binding and revision on every request. Production must apply the additive `provider_source_snapshots` table through the existing [Replit release procedure](../atlas-phase6-6h-release-readiness.md) before turning on the updated build.

Public records in this update include the [Cambria district's payer-specific denial report](https://cambriahealth.specialdistrict.org/files/fca6a82ca/Board%2BAgenda%2BPacket%2BSept%2B24%2C%2B2024.pdf), [Normal's dated inventory](https://www.normalil.gov/Archive/ViewFile/Item/4597) and [purchase approval](https://www.normalil.gov/ArchiveCenter/ViewFile/Item/4635), [Muskegon's AED replacement approval](https://www.muskegon-mi.gov/documents/view/3/3781), and [Santee's monitor replacement approval](https://www.cityofsanteeca.gov/departments/city-clerk/document-central/city-clerk/council-agendas/2025/11-12-2025-agenda-packet.pdf). Cambria's independently reported monitor lease stays separate from owned equipment. The nationwide source coverage export flags dated owned counts, documented purchase and other acquisition dates, payer names in published denial figures, and the source records needed for each profile. The audit covers public profile evidence and excludes private workspace exports.
