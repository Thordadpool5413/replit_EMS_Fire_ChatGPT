# Open EMS inventory and billing sources

Read-only checks performed November 27, 2026. The public requests below used no credentials.

| Source | Verified response | Appropriate profile link |

| [NYC DOHMH AED inventory](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Health/New-York-City-Automated-External-Defibrillator-AED/2er2-jqsx) · [Socrata endpoint](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/resource/2er2-jqsx.json) | A borough aggregation returned 7,437 registry rows. Columns include entity, borough, reported AED count and location. An FDNY name filter returned records for a high school. | Public-access registry context. Match a specifically named site to an agency only with identity and ownership evidence. Citywide registry rows cannot be counted as FDNY equipment. The API lacks device model and purchase date. |
| [Naperville ArcGIS](https://services2.arcgis.com/PYn6bWCjT6bhw1z3/ArcGIS/rest/services/AEDs/FeatureServer/0) and [Detroit ArcGIS](https://services2.arcgis.com/hE9igMm8RoNeQRbh/ArcGIS/rest/services/AED/FeatureServer/0) | Count-only queries returned 2 and 11 records respectively. Layer metadata shows installation date and Naperville operability or Detroit verification fields. These layers lack manufacturer and model fields. | Location evidence within the source scope, with date and location provenance. An installation date does not establish an invoice or payment. Select only required public fields. |
| [Lee County-area ArcGIS registry](https://services2.arcgis.com/LvWGAAhHwbCJ2GMP/ArcGIS/rest/services/Public_AED_Registry_view_VIEWER/FeatureServer/0) | Count-only query returned 104. Schema lists ownership, manufacturer and model. Selected-field and grouped queries returned errors in testing. | Hold integration until a stable permitted query is available and the publishing authority and ownership scope are confirmed. Exclude access codes, personal contacts, serial numbers and free-text notes. |
| [Los Angeles RAMP](https://data.lacity.org/City-Infrastructure-Service-Requests/RAMP-Opportunity-Awards/h8mw-iegk) · [Socrata endpoint](https://data.lacity.org/resource/h8mw-iegk.json) | An award query scoped to Fire returned department, vendor, award date, value and contract number. A defibrillator-specific query returned no rows. | Buyer-linked award research using the existing acquisition job. Preserve award, purchase order, delivery and payment as distinct events. |
| [USAspending](https://api.usaspending.gov/) and existing city adapters | Federal award API publishes awards and recipients. The application already supports federal grants and supplier awards, NYC notices and Chicago purchase orders. | Grant recipient and buying department require separate identity links. A federal vendor award does not establish a department end user. |
| [PulsePoint NEAR Registry](https://www.pulsepoint.org/near-registry) | PulsePoint documents a partner API. Its AED administrative registry requires role-based access. | Evaluate partner authorization and permitted uses. Public locator availability does not supply a no-credential bulk fleet feed. |
| [CMS public provider services](https://data.cms.gov/provider-summary-by-type-of-service/medicare-physician-other-practitioners/medicare-physician-other-practitioners-by-provider-and-service) | The existing adapter retrieves published ambulance codes, usage and Medicare payment fields by exact NPI. | Original Medicare service evidence only. No provider-level denial denominator or payer mix in this dataset. |
| [CMS GADCS](https://www.cms.gov/Medicare/Medicare-Fee-for-Service-Payment/AmbulanceFeeSchedule/Downloads/Medicare-Ground-Ambulance-FAQs.pdf) and [Massachusetts APCD](https://www.chiamass.gov/requesting-submitting-data/requesting-data-records/request-ma-apcd-case-mix-data/) | GADCS publishes aggregate results. Massachusetts requires a data request for APCD extracts. | Geographic or program benchmarks stay at that level. Provider-level extracts require appropriate authorization and scope. |

## Department billing records already mapped

- Naperville’s [2026 city report](https://naperville.legistar.com/ViewReport.ashx?Extra=WithText&GID=682&GUID=D35006D7-4748-4CE3-ACFD-6D42B9E6275F&ID=8027168&M=R&N=Master&Title=Legislation+Details+%28With+Text%29) includes an in-house pilot with 316 administered claims and 47 initial denials. The pilot represents about 20% of billable EMS calls. It also reports billed and collected dollars separately.
- Alameda’s [department study](https://www.alamedaca.gov/files/sharedassets/public/v/1/fire/fire/annual-reports/alameda-crasocstudy-final.pdf) gives 2022 trip shares by payer, plus department transport charges and payments.
- Saint Charles’s [billing RFP addendum](https://stcharlescitymo.gov/DocumentCenter/View/13250/4747-Addendum-1?bidId=) gives the percentage of billed charges for four payer groups in 2022 and 2023. The reported percentages are preserved without redistributing the unreported balance.

Claim counts, trips, billed charges and collections keep their source denominators. For additional municipalities, billing RFPs, council reports, city audits and department standards-of-cover studies are sources of actual named-agency figures. For payer-specific denials and current figures where public evidence is absent, use authorized aggregate billing exports.
