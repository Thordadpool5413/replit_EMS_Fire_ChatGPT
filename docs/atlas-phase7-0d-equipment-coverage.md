# Phase 7.0D — Equipment evidence coverage by category

Adds a read-only selected-state research queue over eligible loaded workspace accounts, with six separate equipment/software categories and mutually exclusive evidence statuses: dated manufacturer text, unresolved manufacturer, reconciliation required, historical only, and no local document. Uses existing source-native identity and exact reviewed-claim gates plus document reconciliation; no inference from awards, search terms, raw inventory fields, quantities, stations or geography. Manufacturer recognition remains contextual, not verification. Existing national research coverage now excludes conflicting reviewed claims. Per-account source links and profile drilldown support user validation. No new external equipment observations were collected or loaded in this phase; no national market share, verified fleet coverage, source completeness or model-level totals claimed. Local evidence only, not shared approval or complete production census. No schema changes, provider records or Replit deployment. Tests contain synthetic fixtures only.
