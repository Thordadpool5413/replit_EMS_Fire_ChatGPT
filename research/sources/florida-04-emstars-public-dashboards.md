## Public Dashboards

Welcome to the Public Dashboards site. Here, you'll find valuable and comprehensive Florida EMS information. These dashboards provide critical insights that are beneficial to everyone, from healthcare professionals to the general public. Explore the data to stay informed about emergency medical services in Florida and how they impact our communities. Your gateway to transparent and accessible EMS information starts here.
