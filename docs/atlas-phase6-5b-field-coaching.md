# Phase 6.5B — Field coaching workspace

Account-specific coaching templates derive from selected-account leadership review prompts and reviewed public evidence. Includes product-specific pre-call questions, purchasing timeline verification, follow-up accountability, opportunity advancement, contact/evidence/location gaps and opportunity qualification. 30-day recorded activity window, at most eight selected accounts and five-minute reviewed sample. Self-review checklist, explicitly confirmed internal action, success measure, owner and review date. Reuses the existing standalone follow-up creation callback and workspace journal/sync; no new backend, automatic tasks, customer contact, stage changes, or rep ratings. No guarantee of cloud acknowledgment; use sync indicator. Unsaved drafts are transient. No team assignment or employee performance score; recommendations are deterministic templates, not generative AI or predictions. End-user validation mandatory. Regression fixtures test-only. Replit deployment user-controlled.
