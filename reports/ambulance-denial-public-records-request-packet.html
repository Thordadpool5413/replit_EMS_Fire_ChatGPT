Common request text

Under Chapter 119, Florida Statutes, please provide existing non-patient-level aggregate reports of Medicare fee-for-service ground-ambulance claim outcomes for calendar years 2024 and 2025, separately, to the extent these records are maintained by or for the County.

Please route this to the ambulance billing/revenue records custodian. This is not a request for a patient's medical record or ambulance bill.

I seek existing reports showing denied claim counts and the corresponding total adjudicated claim counts, by operating ambulance agency/billing NPI and ambulance pickup county, where those groupings already exist. Please keep Medicare Advantage, Medicaid, other payers, and air ambulance services separate. If records use a fiscal year or another existing reporting period, please provide that period as labeled rather than generating a new calendar-year report.

Please include any existing accompanying report definitions or data dictionary identifying:

  • Reporting start/end dates, whether the period is based on service or adjudication date, and report as-of date.
  • Whether the unit is a unique claim, claim line, transport, or service unit.
  • Whether a denial means an initial decision, final post-adjustment/post-appeal disposition, full denial, or partial denial; please keep these distinct where the existing report does.
  • The denominator's inclusion/exclusion of pending, rejected/unadjudicated, adjusted, and reversed claims, and existing duplicate-handling definitions.
  • Billing NPI-to-operating-agency relationships, pickup-county attribution method, covered providers, and documented exclusions. An office mailing address should not be interpreted as a pickup county.

Electronic CSV/XLSX is preferred if already available; an existing PDF report is acceptable. Please preserve suppression of small cells as needed. Do not provide patient-level files (even redacted), names, Medicare beneficiary numbers, dates of birth, street-level pickup addresses, claim identifiers, EOBs, 835 files, or clinical records.

This request is limited to existing records, not a demand for new analysis, custom aggregation, or a new report. Collection percentages, unpaid balances, contractual write-offs, billed-dollar adjustments, and audit-sample error rates should not be relabeled as claim-denial counts. If the requested count report is not held, please say so and provide an existing report catalog or relevant custodian contact if readily available.

No fees, paid research, custom programming, deposits, or chargeable work are authorized. If fulfillment would incur any charge, please provide an itemized estimate and wait for separate written approval before proceeding. Please reply electronically to this sending mailbox with responsive public records or a download link.

Thank you.

How a response can support the app

Actual claim-denial percentage = 100 × denied claims ÷ adjudicated claims. Agency records need a confirmed billing-NPI relationship. County records need pickup-county attribution. Combining reports requires matching payer, period, counting unit, denial definition, decision point, and non-overlapping coverage.

A county operation's report does not automatically cover every private or municipal provider in the county. These three operations are not complete Florida coverage. Partial provider cohorts must stay labeled as partial cohorts; suppressed or missing cells are not zeros. No state-wide percentage can be inferred from these requests alone.

This document is a submission/status record and request draft, not a denial-rate dataset. Replies are directed to the sending Outlook mailbox. No background reply monitoring or automatic ingestion has been configured.
