---
name: Equipment and offer evidence boundaries
description: Distinguish installed-product records, contracted suppliers, and agency-specific financial offers.
---

Do not identify an agency's contracted supplier from a manufacturer, brand match, missing equipment value, or agency demographics. Manufacturer and supplier are different facts.

**Why:** Earlier profile logic guessed distributors from population parity and suppliers from brands, making equipment research look more certain than its records supported.

**How to apply:** Display recorded equipment values as working references, but keep actual supplier/service-provider fields explicit and source-backed. Missing supplier records must stay Not reported rather than implying a known distributor or no installed device.

Buy-back cash, replacement-purchase credit, and illustrative trade-in assumptions are distinct. A fleet inventory is not an eligible-quantity confirmation.

**Why:** The mobile profile prioritizes equipment identification and buy-back knowledge; treating planning benchmarks as written offers would mislead purchasing conversations.

**How to apply:** Require a recorded device, explicit currency, quoted per-unit amount, and whole eligible quantity before presenting a quoted fleet total. Never substitute ambulance count, full installed quantity, or a default competitor match. Retain quote dates and conditions, and never count the same credit twice as savings.

Make responsive layouts depend on the available component width, not only the screen width.

**Why:** Enlarged profile text can narrow a brief inside a desktop viewport while desktop multi-column finding rules still apply.

**How to apply:** Reflow findings, metric groups, and equipment rows naturally under enlarged text. Do not impose fixed-height caps or shrink essential figures to conceal overflow.
