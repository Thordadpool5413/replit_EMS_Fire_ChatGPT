# Phase 6.4L — Equipment replacement research

Extends the existing authenticated provider lifecycle view with all-maker station research groups and 30/90/180/365-day windows for reported warranty/service dates. Distinguishes upcoming, historical, conflicting, missing and later dates. Acquisition is historical context only. Requires a unique source-native identity, exact NERIS parent, complete accepted station sample, five-minute freshness, unique IDs, valid dates, safe source URLs and matching state. Direct observations remain explicitly field observations. Dates are not replacement predictions; groups are not unique devices. No extrapolated fleet counts, linked capital purchases, automatic outreach, tasks, sales stage or production data writes. Department purchasing/funding context remains separate in the existing lifecycle view. Test fixtures only; deployment is user-controlled.
