# Phase 8.0D — Equipment procurement evidence acquisition

Uses existing validated public NYC City Record and Chicago purchase-order connectors, 365 and 1095-day windows respectively. Five topics and maximum three pages each (30 total requests). Retains source-native notice or purchase-order revision identity, date, issuing organization, vendor and original link. Cross-topic exact duplicates collapse; conflicting rows are withheld. Explicit product/manufacturer text is separate from vendor-name mentions. No inference of purchase, installation, quantity, active fleet, current decision-maker, end user or provider identity. Records are review-only JSON and hash summary artifacts, not automatic app publication. Source failures and truncated scopes are explicit. No national coverage claim or installed fleet acquisition; independent official inventory confirmation remains outstanding. No Replit or production changes.
