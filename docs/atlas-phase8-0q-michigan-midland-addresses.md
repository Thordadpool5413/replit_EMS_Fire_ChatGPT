# Phase 8.0Q: Michigan municipal GIS recovery

Michigan's statewide NG911 repository is restricted to authorized public safety entities and service providers: https://www.michigan.gov/dtmb/services/maps/michigan-statewide-ng911-gis-repository . Do not attempt access to private statewide layers. Use the public City of Midland service https://arcgis1.midland-mi.org/arcgis/rest/services/CityCountyShared/FeatureServer/1 with pinned item and observed field schema and lifecycle domain. The committed MI snapshot contains 1,373 source identities and 417 no-match/unavailable records; three meet the county or postal-city research filter. This is a small municipal pilot, not statewide coverage. Require exact street, postal city, state, ZIP, active municipal address and unique object. Preserve registry county only as reported (the layer does not independently establish county); placement and provider operation remain unknown. Review-only artifacts, no automatic map publication, no Replit changes. CI fixtures are not production data.
