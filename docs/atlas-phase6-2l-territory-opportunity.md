# Phase 6.2L - territory opportunity research

Builds on the staged 6.2K station map. Select up to five linked departments; reviewed station equipment evidence stays separate from reviewed parent-level public procurement, grant, capital funding and contract documents. The existing shared evidence workspace membership and complete reviewed radar checks are reused. Explicit research order: dated open procurement or renewal, documented funding, field equipment confirmation, award follow-up, research gap. Dates are source deadlines only, never invented replacement dates. Equipment category and age filters affect station observations, not parent documents. Unknown, unavailable, and duplicate records are not competitor signals. No revenue forecasts, probability score, pipeline writes, county assignment, purchase claims or automatic validation. The sample expires with the station map; private read-only checks, no new schema. Production deployment and CI must be verified. This is selected-account research, not nationwide coverage.
