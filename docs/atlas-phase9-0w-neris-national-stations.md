# Phase 9.0W: national NERIS department and station acquisition

USFA publishes current NERIS public department and station layers at https://www.usfa.fema.gov/neris/public/ . The legacy registry is frozen at the June 2026 transition; do not describe it as current. Reuse the earlier NERIS native relationship validator, but replace the three-state research scope with a complete source object inventory and bounded 200-object queries. Retain exact native parent/child IDs, source dates, department headquarters and station address fields, object IDs, WGS84 coordinates as reported, discrepancies, cross-state stations and shared-coordinate flags. Validate both layers' service item, schema, relationship, count, ID inventory and metadata before and after download. Native source relationships require no state licensing check. Keep fire organization identities separate from FDIDs/NPIs until user confirms a cross-registry match. The full source is not a complete list of all US stations. No automatic mapping counts or Replit changes. Acquisition runs separately from merge; actual source records and counts must come from the successful artifact.
