# Equipment map interpretation

The old national layer used a categorical presence rule: any ZOLL record plus any competitor record colored the entire state purple. It did not measure dominance or market share. The legend used the same color constants, but geographic opacity fading could alter their appearance and similar neutral colors made missing data and filter misses difficult to distinguish.

The national equipment view now keeps state geometry neutral and places a proportional bar at each state label, including small-state callouts. The bar partitions county-assigned agency profiles into ZOLL only, competitor only, both at the same agency, unrecognized manufacturer, and no equipment entry. The inspector and county equipment panel show the corresponding counts and percentages. Color values come from the same constants as the legend, and equipment bars are not faded for the region filter.

Selecting a vendor changes match counts, not the composition denominator. Mixed agencies occupy one category rather than being counted twice. Generated planning prospects without verified agency identity or substantive equipment evidence are excluded from equipment summaries. Registry-only listings, unresolved county assignments and unresearched agencies are outside the percentage denominator and remain explicitly described as coverage gaps.

These percentages describe loaded agency records. They are not unit-based installed market share, verified geographic territory, or a complete census of statewide agency ownership. Neutral state geometry remains neutral even when every loaded profile names one vendor.
